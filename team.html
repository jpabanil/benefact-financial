Founder & owner

Christopher Wilson

I don’t just sell insurance. I build financial fortresses. My career began in collections and tax collections, where I saw firsthand how quickly a life or a business can be derailed by unexpected medical debt and poor planning. Today I use that inside view so clients do not end up there.

Blending that background with life and health certifications — Medicare, the ACA marketplace, and employer group benefits — I help people and businesses find the missing pieces in the financial puzzle.

  • Healthcare: New York State of Health, the ACA marketplace, and Medicare, for individuals and groups.
  • Tax-advantaged growth: life insurance with living benefits used as a planning tool.
  • Employer solutions: $0 net-cost healthcare strategies through the Vital 110 plan.
  • Estate planning: keeping a legacy from being eaten by unnecessary taxes and liabilities.

I care about the balance between premium cost and out-of-pocket protection. If you want a straight audit of what you have now, call or write.
