Major medical and ACA group health

We guide businesses through both the state small-group market and national group markets.

New York State of Health, small business marketplace

For New York employers we use the official Small Business Marketplace, including the small-business healthcare tax credit when the group qualifies, and we select from leading regional carrier networks.

Multi-state and national groups

Operating outside New York, or managing people in more than one state? We structure group health for businesses under Healthcare.gov and state-based exchanges so the plan stays compliant with local rules and essential health benefits.

Claims protection, the “deductible buster”

Pairing a major medical plan with the Vital 110 plan keeps day-to-day claims — doctor visits, labs, routine prescriptions — off the primary health policy. Shifting that routine use away from the major plan is how Benefact helps a business avoid a painful renewal increase.

Voluntary and supplemental group coverage

Round out the package with employee-paid or employer-sponsored options:

  • Group dental and vision. Preventive cleanings, eye exams, and hardware, on flexible networks.
  • Short-term and long-term disability. Income protection for a temporary absence or a long one.
  • Group life and AD&D. A base of financial security for employees and their families.
  • Accident and critical illness. Lump-sum cash when a serious health event creates bills the major medical plan does not pay first.
  • Hospital indemnity. A fixed daily benefit during a hospital stay, useful against a high deductible.

Tax-advantaged savings

Programs such as Section 125 payroll optimization, including the Vital 110 plan, can be used in any state. The design reduces employer FICA taxes and can deliver $0 copay benefits to staff. Employers in Benefact’s Vital 110 design save $438 per employee per year in FICA while the workforce receives the healthcare benefit without a new net expense for the employee or the company.
