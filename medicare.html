Medicare

A clear path onto Medicare, and a plan that fits your doctors.

Advantage, Medigap, and Part D are easy to mix up. We slow the decision down and match coverage to your prescriptions, your physicians, and the way you actually get care.

Request Medicare help Talk with Christopher

Transitioning into Medicare, or revisiting a supplement you already have, gets overwhelming fast. Benefact provides step-by-step education so the plan fits your lifestyle, your doctors, and your prescriptions.

Moving from individual ACA coverage or from an employer plan requires the same care. As a certified Medicare broker, we help with:

  • Medicare Advantage (Part C) and Medicare Supplement (Medigap)
  • Prescription drug plans (Part D)
  • Dual eligible support for people who have Medicare and Medicaid

We do not offer every plan available in your area. Any Medicare decision should be checked against Medicare.gov or 1-800-MEDICARE, and against your local State Health Insurance Assistance Program. Benefact is not connected with or endorsed by the federal Medicare program.

What we sort out with you

Doctors and hospitals

Advantage plans use networks. Medigap plans generally do not. We start with the physicians and facilities you want to keep.

Prescriptions

Part D formularies change. We look up the drugs you take before recommending a plan, instead of after a pharmacy surprise.

Timing

Initial enrollment, employer coverage ending, and the annual election period each have their own rules. We tell you which window you are in.
