Meet the founder

Christopher started in collections and tax collections, where he saw how fast a family or a business can be knocked off course by medical debt. He built Benefact so clients would have someone in their corner before that happens.

He is licensed for life and health, including Medicare, the ACA marketplace, and employer group benefits. The work is finding the missing piece: the right network, the right premium, or a benefits design that actually fits the payroll.

For employers

The Vital 110 plan

Health Compass Inc.’s Vital 110 plan, offered through Benefact, is built for groups of 10 or more. It uses a Section 125 cafeteria plan so routine care can be funded from payroll-tax savings. Benefact’s figures: $438 in FICA savings per employee per year, with $0 copays on the everyday benefits in the design.
